## Interpretation and caveats

- When stand is partly genetic (h2_stand > 0, r_g != 0), the phenotypic adjustment also removes or adds genetic variance (delta_g). delta_e then overstates the gain in selection accuracy; the relevant quantity is the change in h2_w (or Vg/Ve). The within-genotype adjustment removes only non-genetic stand variation and is the cleaner estimate of an error-variance reduction that leaves Vg intact.
- `plants_harvested` is a harvest count, not an emergence count. It reflects emergence plus later plant loss to disease, drought, lodging and damage, which are partly genotype dependent (vigor, disease resistance). An emergence count taken by smartphone early in the season would capture the seed and establishment component and would differ in both its error-reducing power and its genetic content.
- Plot size is 4.5 m2 with about 20 to 75 harvested plants; results apply to similar small-plot preliminary yield trials.
