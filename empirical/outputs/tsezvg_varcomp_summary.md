## Data and cleaning

- Rows read: 7631 plots in 33 trials. Genotype = GID (one GID per DESIGNATION and vice versa; no name harmonization needed). Environment = trial (Trial Code; the file gives the year but not the season).
- GY is analyzed as recorded (kg/ha; trial means 158-2,217). How GY was scaled from plot weight is not documented in the file.
- Excluded: missing GY 287 plots (including all 30 plots of trial 25, which has no GY data); residual outliers (|standardized conditional residual| > 3.5 from yield ~ rep + (1|genotype) per trial) 9 plots.
- Growth habit (GHT) is constant within trials: 21 bush and 11 climbing trials analyzed. Incomplete blocks are numbered within replicate (rep:block).

## Yield units and design (checked against the source paper)
Saradadevi et al. (2021), Plant Genome 14:e20156, Section 2.1:
- **Design:** trials were alpha-lattice designs with two or three complete blocks subdivided into incomplete sub-blocks.
- **Plots:** three rows 3 m long and 50 cm apart, effective plot area 4.5 m², 100 seeds sown for a target of 90 plants.
- **Yield:** seed was dried to about 13% moisture and weighed, and the weight was converted to GY using the effective plot area.

So GY is an area-based yield in kg/ha, and REP_NO/BLOCK_NO correspond to complete blocks and incomplete sub-blocks, as modeled.
