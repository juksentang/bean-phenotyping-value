## 3. Genotype (designation) harmonization

- Distinct raw strings: 848; distinct harmonized names: 587.

- Genotypes with more than two plots in one trial (repeated check entries, e.g. CAL96 entered twice as `CAL96` and `CAL 96`): 19 genotype-trial combinations; their plots are kept and pooled under one genotype.

## 4. Yield variable and unit checks

- In every export, YDPL = GMPLOT / PLPLOT (g per plant) and recorded YDHA = 200 x YDPL, i.e. yield per plant scaled to 200,000 plants/ha (kg/ha), not plot weight divided by plot area. `yield_kg_ha` is recomputed as 200 x GMPLOT / PLPLOT for every plot; this is the variable analyzed by Amongi et al. (YDHA).
- Files where recorded YDHA departs from 200 x GMPLOT / PLPLOT (all other files agree to rounding):
  - `DAB Set3 (DABs PYT) 2018B_Kitengule.xls`: recorded / recomputed median 10.000 (range 10.00-10.00); recorded trial mean 19364 kg/ha.
  - `Drought Tolerant Coloured Beans PYT 2015B-Kawanda.xlsx`: recorded / recomputed median 1.017 (range 0.15-1.37); recorded trial mean 1581 kg/ha.
  - DAB Set3 2018B Kitengule: recorded YDHA = 2000 x YDPL (mean ~19,400 kg/ha), a factor-10 scaling error; YDPL, GMPLOT and PLPLOT are on the same scale as the other Kitengule trials, so the recomputed value (mean ~1,940 kg/ha) is used.
  - Drought Tolerant Coloured Beans 2015B Kawanda: recorded YDHA = GMPLOT x 10 / 3 (area-based on a 3 m2 plot) rather than per-plant based; recomputed on the per-plant scale used by all other exports.
- 4 plots have YDHA but no GMPLOT or PLPLOT; recorded YDHA kept (their files are on the standard 200 x YDPL scale).
- A second, area-based yield `yield_area_kg_ha` = GMPLOT (g) x 10 / plot area (m2; 4.5 m2 in every file that reports it) is also written for sensitivity analysis. It does not correct for stand and is not affected by the per-plant extrapolation.
