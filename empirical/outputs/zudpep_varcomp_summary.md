## Multi-environment estimates per trial series

Model: yield ~ env + (1|genotype) + (1|genotype:env) + (1|env:rep) + (1|env:rep:block), REML. Point estimates use a residual variance per environment (glmmTMB, `dispformula = ~ env`) where it converged, with Ve = mean of the environment residual variances; 95% intervals are parametric-bootstrap percentiles (200 simulations from, and refits of, the same model; the interval for kappa is derived from that of rho_env, since kappa = 1/rho_env - 1). The homogeneous-residual lme4 estimates are in the CSV columns `*_hom`. Sets are connected components of trials sharing >= 10 genotypes and >= 50% of the smaller entry list; each component is exactly one trial series. Eligibility: >= 3 environments and >= 10 genotypes common to all of them.
